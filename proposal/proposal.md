# DSC 106 Final Project Proposal

## Project Title
Climate Change: Regional Temperature Trends and Future Projections

## Dataset
CMIP6 (Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6) - CESM2-WACCM (r1i1p1f1)

## Research Question
How have temperature trends varied across world regions, and which regions are projected to experience the greatest warming in the future?

## Brief Writeup
Our project investigates regional variations in historical and projected temperature changes using CMIP6 data from the CESM2-WACCM climate model. We analyze historical simulations (1950–2014) and future projections (2020–2099) under the SSP5-8.5 emissions scenario, focusing on geographic differences rather than comparisons across scenarios. Our analysis examines five continents: North America, South America, Europe, Africa, and Asia (Europe and Asia are split at the Ural Mountains, ~60°E). Using line charts, bar charts, heatmaps, geographic maps, dumbbell charts, and scatter plots, we compare historical warming patterns, projected temperature increases, and changes in warming rates. We also investigate the relationship between latitude and projected warming, particularly at higher northern latitudes. Our final interactive website will allow users to select regions and explore temperature changes over time. Our goal is to communicate regional climate differences through spatial and temporal analysis. Anomalies are measured against a 1951–1980 baseline. As our analysis uses a single climate model (CESM2-WACCM, a high-sensitivity model) and a high-emissions scenario, the results are model-specific and at the upper end of CMIP6 projections.
