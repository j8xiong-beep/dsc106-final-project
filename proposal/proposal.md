# DSC 106 Final Project Proposal

## Project Title
Climate Change: Global Temperature Trends and Future Predictions

## Dataset
CMIP6 (Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6)

## Research Question
How have temperature trends varied across world regions, and which regions are projected to experience the greatest warming in the future?

## Brief Writeup
Our project explores how climate change affects different regions around the world. We will use CMIP6 temperature data to compare historical temperature trends and future projections. Rather than focusing on different emission scenarios, we want to explore how warming varies geographically. We will examine regions such as North America, South America, Europe, Africa, and Asia. Our visualizations will use maps, line charts, bar charts, heatmaps, and scatter plots to show different patterns of temperature change. We also want to investigate whether higher-latitude regions experience greater warming. For our final interactive website, users will be able to select regions and explore temperature changes over time. Our goal is to make regional climate differences easier to understand and identify which regions may experience the greatest future warming.

## Planned Visualizations
1. Global Temperature Change Map
2. Historical Temperature Trends by Region
3. Regional Warming Ranking
4. Temperature Changes Across Decades
5. Future Regional Temperature Projections
6. Latitude vs. Temperature Change
